      Limits & allowance

      How much Preen may send, and how fast it works. The limits apply to every request: automatic runs, right-click, folders and the toolbar button. 0 means no limit.

      Limits are checked before each message; requests already in progress may finish after a limit is reached.

      Speed

      No speed limit: Preen works as fast as it can, three messages at a time. Not recommended for large folders or slower computers.

      For large folders, Medium or Low is recommended: runs take longer but the computer stays responsive.

      Accounts

      For each account: which folders Preen processes automatically, and how much of each message it sends.

      Special folders (Trash, Junk, Sent, Drafts, Outbox, Templates, Archives) are never processed automatically.

      Used by accounts set to “Use default”.

      With Deep (the default), about 2,000 characters of each message’s text are sent to your provider. Choose Surface to send no message text at all.

      Junk & sensitive info

      Both are off by default.

      Junk

      Higher: fewer, safer marks. Marking real mail as junk is the costly mistake, so the default is high.

      Thunderbird hasn’t allowed Preen to move mail yet, so junk stays where it is.

      Sensitive info

      Categories

      Jev picks the category whose description fits best, so the descriptions matter more than the names. Use Is to say what belongs in a category, and Is not for similar mail that belongs somewhere else. Certainty required sets how sure Preen must be before tagging.

      Categories are off. Preen gives no category label and doesn't ask for one. The yes/no checks you turned on (real person, needs reply, to-do, junk) still run, so Preen can be used for junk alone.

      Categories pick one label per message; questions add yes/no tags. The questions are set under People, replies & to-dos and Junk & sensitive info.

      Privacy & data

      Sent with every request. Uses the same character limit as categories.

      Sent with every request. Empty: nothing is sent.

      What Jev sees: the sender, subject, date, number of recipients and attachment names; header hints (such as a list-unsubscribe header); the kind of mailbox and your note about it; your personal context; the languages you read; and the message text, depending on Depth. Your account name and address are never sent.

      Deep sends about 2,000 characters of message text; Surface never sends any.

      Monthly token summaries are kept even after old entries are removed.

      Tag corrections

      When you add or remove a Preen tag yourself, Preen notes the tag, the change and its own score, on this computer only (no sender, subject or text). They are kept 2 years, apart from the history, so Clear history doesn't remove them.

      Statistics

      Your totals

      Counted on this computer from the activity history. Only you see these.

      Undo

      Puts the messages from Preen's most recent applied run back how they were. Tags or changes you made yourself are left alone. Older runs can be undone from the Activity log.

      Backup

      Save your settings to a file, or load them from one. Imported settings are shown here for review and only take effect when you click Save.

      The file holds your key in plain text. Anyone with it can use your key.
